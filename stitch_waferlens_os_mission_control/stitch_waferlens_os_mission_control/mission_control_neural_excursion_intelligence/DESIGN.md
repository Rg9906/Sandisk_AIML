## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-density, mission-critical operational interface for semiconductor yield analytics and cleanroom robotics orchestration. It fuses three primary aesthetics: the high-stakes situational awareness of aerospace mission operations, the tactical hyper-precision of a cyberpunk cleanroom telemetry HUD, and the spatial data efficiency of financial terminals.

The emotional core is absolute certainty under pressure: zero latency, hyper-legible telemetry, optical precision, and crystalline structural isolation. Every element communicates sub-micron scale tolerances. Visual noise is eliminated; contrast is reserved strictly for operational state transitions, thermal anomalies, and nanometer defect classifications.

Visual characteristics include:
- Deep optical black baselines reminiscent of deep cleanroom lithography suites and orbital control screens.
- Specular glass surfaces with laser-milled 1px luminous boundary lines.
- Orthogonal HUD reticles, crosshairs, precision coordinate brackets, and telemetry micro-ticks.
- Luminescent status signaling mapped directly to yield severity and neural anomaly gradients.

## Colors

The palette operates under an uncompromising dark-mode mandate to eliminate ocular fatigue in low-ambient cleanroom operations and optimize luminescence contrast ratios.

### Base Structural Tones
- **Deep Obsidian Black (`#05080E`)**: Primary viewport canvas background. Absolute visual foundation.
- **Silicon Indigo Deep (`#080E1A`)**: Secondary canvas base and nested pane recesses.
- **Silicon Indigo Glow (`#0B1528`)**: Raised panel base, inactive tracking rails, and muted data backdrops.
- **Glassmorphic Slate (`rgba(16, 25, 40, 0.72)`)**: Surface container for operational telemetry cards and floating HUD modules.
- **Specular Border Cyan (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.22)`)**: Structural 1px precision boundary trace.

### Functional Luminescence & Telemetry Accents
- **Electric Neon Cyan (`#00F0FF`)**: Primary telemetry cursor, active toolpath traces, focal reticles, and system-level normal states.
- **Crystalline Mint Emerald (`#00E676`)**: Nominal yield thresholds, verified wafer lots, operational cleanroom flow.
- **Radiant Thermal Amber (`#FFB300`)**: Neural excursion warnings, thermal drift alerts, marginal wafer drift.
- **Laser Ruby (`#FF1744`)**: Critical contamination events, yield crashes, lithography stage fault interlocks.
- **Holographic Plasma Violet (`#B388FF`)**: Neural inference overlays, machine learning confidence masks, defect clustering models.

### Text & Contrast Hierarchy
- **Text Ultra-High (`#F0F6FC`)**: Critical numeric readouts, active status headers, primary coordinates.
- **Text High (`#C9D1D9`)**: Standard labels, operational body data.
- **Text Dim / Micro (`#586069`)**: Grid indices, unit abbreviations, non-critical telemetry frames.

## Typography

The typography architecture balances instantaneous visual hierarchy with high-density quantitative tracking:

- **Headlines (`Outfit`)**: High-contrast geometric structure providing authoritative command hierarchy. Uppercase treatment is used for station labels, lot status codes, and terminal zones.
- **Body (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Clean, crisp, neutral readability for operational descriptions, operator logs, and protocol runbooks.
- **Telemetry & Indicators (`JetBrains Mono`)**: Strict tabular alignment. All numeric indices, die coordinates, stage temperatures, and yield metrics require fixed-width figures (`tnum`, `zero`) to prevent layout shifts during real-time streaming intervals.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial model uses an uncompromising ultra-dense modular grid engineered for high-resolution industrial viewports (4K/Ultrawide cleanroom wall monitors) while adapting systematically to field tablets.

- **Dense Micro-Rhythm**: Built on a base 4px/8px micro-grid. In-card telemetry elements utilize `space-xs` (2px) and `space-sm` (4px) to sustain maximum information density without optical collision.
- **Mission Control Tiling**: Multi-pane layout modeled after flight director consoles. Screen real estate is divided into pinned persistent headers (system health/FAB interlocks), a central telemetry/wafer-map visualization stage, and dual collapsable operational rail drawers (neural analytics, parameter streams).
- **Breakpoint Behavior**:
  - **Desktop / Ultra-Dense HUD (1440px+)**: Multi-column docked tiled arrangement with non-overlapping viewports, sub-pixel grid lines, and 12px gutters.
  - **Tablet Console (768px - 1439px)**: Dual-pane layout; collapsible left nav rail, secondary telemetry auto-parks into stacked drawer tabs.
  - **Mobile Interlock (Under 768px)**: Single primary viewport with vertical card stacking, horizontal scrolling sparklines, and thumb-accessible bottom interlock controls.

## Elevation & Depth

Elevation does not rely on soft drop shadows. It uses luminous optical depths, physical glass refraction, and specular boundary layers to simulate a multi-layered holographic flight instrument.

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: Pure Obsidian `#05080E` with optional 24px micro-grid wireframe at 4% cyan opacity.
- **Level 1 (Sub-Panels & Structural Modules)**: `rgba(11, 21, 40, 0.85)` surface with a 1px perimeter trace of `rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.12)`.
- **Level 2 (Glass Floating Cards & HUD Modules)**: Glassmorphic Slate `rgba(16, 25, 40, 0.72)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a directional top-edge highlight (`linear-gradient(180deg, rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.3) 0%, rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08) 100%)`).
- **Level 3 (Target Reticles & Excursion Alerts)**: High-luminescence state. Back-projected radial glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.25)`. Critical fault states project a laser-ruby halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 30px -2px rgba(255, 23, 68, 0.35)`.
- **Corner Brackets**: Key operational panels feature L-shaped 4px geometric corner crosshairs in cyan to simulate precision optical sightlines.

## Shapes

The design system employs a tightly disciplined, precision-milled shape aesthetic. Organic curves are rejected in favor of technical, low-radius chamfers and crisp edges.

- **Base Radius (0.25rem / 4px)**: Applied to inner cards, telemetry badges, data cells, and interactive modules.
- **Container Radius (0.5rem / 8px)**: Applied to main viewport bays and floating glass HUD windows.
- **Corner Notches**: Select status cards and master control trigger buttons feature a 45-degree chamfered top-right or bottom-left corner (4px - 6px cut) to reinforce industrial avionics heritage.

## Components

### Buttons & Interlocks
- **Primary Cyber Interlock**: Neon Cyan `#00F0FF` background, obsidian `#05080E` text in `Outfit Bold`, 4px radius with an acute corner-notch. Hover initiates high-intensity edge luminescence (`0 0 16px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.6)`).
- **Secondary Telemetry Action**: Glassmorphic slate background, 1px specular cyan border, `JetBrains Mono` uppercase label. Active press yields an inward silicon-indigo inset depression.
- **Hazard / Override Action**: Laser Ruby `#FF1744` stroke, transparent fill with red scanline pulse animation during active fault confirmation.

### Telemetry Badges & Chips
- Ultra-compact, pill-free badges with 2px corner radius.
- Constructed with a 15% opacity tint of the status color, a solid 1px matching border, and high-intensity monospace status text (e.g., `LOT-8829: NORMAL` in mint emerald, `DEFECT: EXCURSION` in radiant amber).

### Data Tables & Die Matrix Lists
- Alternating rows using `rgba(11, 21, 40, 0.4)` and transparent backdrops.
- 1px hairline horizontal separators (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`).
- All quantitative fields right-aligned with fixed JetBrains Mono telemetry typography.
- Active row selection designated via an illuminated vertical neon cyan pip on the row's left border.

### Precision Input & Stage Tuning Controls
- Inactive input fields share the dark slate glass aesthetic with recessed inner borders.
- Focused inputs illuminate with a 1px Electric Cyan boundary and localized corner brackets.
- Stepper values and parameter inputs feature tactile micro-increment buttons labeled in monospace font.

### Wafer Reticle HUD Cards
- Specialized container housing circular silicon wafer visualizations and die-defect density heatmaps.
- Framed by external coordinate markers (`X: 124.08`, `Y: -45.19`), integrated crosshair overlay traces, and specular top specular highlights simulating an ultra-clean glass chamber portal.
